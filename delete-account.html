Delete your PRISMBURST account

This page is for players of PRISMBURST — Brick Odyssey (Android app com.hamzaxali.prismburst and the web game). It explains how to delete your account and the data linked to it.

Option 1: in the game

  1. Open PRISMBURST and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account. Sign in if you are asked to.
  3. Type DELETE and tap Delete my account forever.

You can also open Account from the home screen while signed in and tap Delete account.

Option 2: on this page

Sign in with the same account you use in the game, then confirm below.

Accounts are not set up on this copy of the site, so there is nothing stored online to delete.

Forgot the password? Use Forgot password? in the game first. Facebook sign-in is not available.

Signed in.

Deleting removes your account, your display name and your cloud save. It cannot be undone.

Your account and its cloud save were deleted.

This browser may still have game progress saved for the web game. It is not removed unless you choose to.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account: email address, sign-in provider and the provider's account id.
  • Your display name.
  • Your cloud save (levels, stars, coins, skins, settings and stats).

Deletion happens straight away in the live database. Nothing is kept for advertising or analytics, because the game has neither. Routine backups held by the hosting provider (Supabase), if any, are overwritten on their normal schedule and are not used to restore deleted accounts.

What is not deleted

Progress saved on your phone stays on your phone. In the game, after deleting, you can choose Also clear this device's progress, or use Settings → Reset progress at any time. Uninstalling the Android app also removes it.

Cannot sign in?

Open an issue at github.com/HamzaXali/prismburst-brick-odyssey/issues titled “Account deletion request”. Do not post your password or email address there; the developer will reply with a private way to confirm the account.
